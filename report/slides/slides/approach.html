Approach

Two ways to featurize legal text, one classifier

LDA + Logistic Regression

Finds abstract recurring topics across all rulings (gensim, TF-IDF). Each ruling becomes a mix-of-topics score. A classifier maps topic mix → category.

Tuned: 100 topics (vs. 50 default)

Doc2Vec + Logistic Regression

Learns a dense numeric "fingerprint" per ruling by predicting words in context (gensim Doc2Vec). Same classifier maps fingerprint → category.

Tuned: 50-dim vectors (vs. 100 default)

A third method (BERT) was in the original paper, but it ran out of memory there — no baseline to replicate.
