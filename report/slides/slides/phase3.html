Phase 3 — Beyond the Paper

Same features, different classifier on top

The paper only tries Logistic Regression. We also tried SVM, Random Forest, and Naive Bayes — same Doc2Vec fingerprints, issue_area task.

Classifier Test Acc Test Macro-F1
SVM (RBF kernel)68.8%0.499
Logistic Regression62.2%0.477
Random Forest59.6%0.317
Naive Bayes58.7%0.462

SVM wins — its RBF kernel draws a curved decision boundary through the embedding space that straight-line Logistic Regression can't match.
