A Closer Look

LDA guesses the majority class. Doc2Vec doesn't.

LDA

Macro-F1 (0.267) trails accuracy (48.7%) badly — it defaults to the two biggest categories (Criminal Procedure, Economic Activity) and misses rare ones almost entirely (Privacy, Attorneys, Interstate Relations: near 0 recall).

Doc2Vec

Far more balanced — still finds minority categories like Privacy (F1 0.26) and Attorneys (F1 0.31) that LDA completely misses, because it reads context, not just word counts.

Same pattern the original paper reports on its own category breakdown.
